About Me

Dedicated to improving digital experiences.

Hi! I’m Maarten de Klerk, a dedicated graphic designer based in South Limburg, the Netherlands. I am currently a student at PXL University of Applied Sciences and Arts in Hasselt, where I am pushing my creative boundaries and sharpening my technical skills.

I have a strong passion for building visual identities and modern digital designs. I love translating complex ideas into clear, impactful visuals that perfectly align with a client's vision. As a versatile and flexible designer, I am always open to new challenges and a wide variety of projects.
